This section contains a collection of blog posts that I have published on the [AWS Study Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/awsstudygroupfcj). Each article summarizes my learning journey while exploring AWS services, cloud architecture, security, and AI-related technologies, along with my own observations and reflections.

### [Blog 1 - Exploring AI Traffic Analysis Dashboards in AWS WAF](3.1-Blog1/)

The rapid growth of AI systems such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity has introduced a new category of web traffic known as **AI traffic**. This creates new challenges in monitoring, resource optimization, and application security. In this blog, I explore **AI Traffic Analysis Dashboards**, a new feature in AWS WAF that helps identify, analyze, and monitor requests generated by AI crawlers. I also share my thoughts on how cloud security services are evolving to adapt to the increasing adoption of AI technologies.

### [Blog 2 - AWS Shield Advanced Attack Flow Logs for DDoS Attack Monitoring](3.2-Blog2/)

This blog introduces a new enhancement to **Amazon EKS Pod Identity** that adds support for **session policies**, allowing IAM permissions to be scoped more precisely for individual pods without creating multiple IAM roles. This feature helps organizations implement the principle of least privilege more effectively in large-scale Kubernetes environments while simplifying permission management.

### [Blog 3 - Modernizing KYC with Serverless & Agentic AI](3.3-Blog3/)

Artificial intelligence is transforming how financial institutions perform **Know Your Customer (KYC)** processes, from identity verification to fraud detection and risk assessment. Recently, AWS and IBM introduced a reference architecture that combines **Agentic AI**, **Event-Driven Architecture**, and AWS managed services to modernize KYC workflows. While the proposed architecture presents many valuable design patterns, it also raises important questions about real-world implementation, including regulatory compliance, security considerations, operational costs, and architectural assumptions. In this blog, I summarize the key concepts presented in the original article and share my own analysis of the technical and practical challenges that should be considered before deploying such a solution in production.
