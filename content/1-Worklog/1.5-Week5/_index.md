### Week 5 Achievements:

* Understand the operation of IAM Roles and temporary credential mechanisms.
* Master why AWS recommends using IAM Roles instead of Access Keys on EC2.
* Understand the relationship between IAM Role, Policy, and EC2 Instance Profile.
* Understand that AWS Cloud9 is an integrated development environment (IDE) running directly on the web.
* Master the process of creating, managing, and using Cloud9 IDE, Terminal, and AWS CLI.
