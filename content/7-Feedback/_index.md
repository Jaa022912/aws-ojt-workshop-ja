# Sharing and Feedback

## General Evaluation

### 1. Working Environment

The working environment was friendly, professional, and highly supportive. Everyone was willing to help whenever I encountered difficulties, making it easy for me to adapt and stay motivated throughout the internship. The atmosphere encouraged learning, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

### 2. Support from Mentors and Program Administrators

This was the aspect I appreciated the most during the program. My mentor always responded quickly to my questions, provided detailed explanations, and patiently guided me whenever I encountered technical challenges. The FCAJ administration team also offered clear instructions, learning materials, and timely support, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable learning experience.

### 3. Relevance to My Academic Major

The assigned tasks were closely related to my academic background while also introducing me to new areas such as Cloud Computing and AWS services. These experiences expanded my knowledge beyond what I had learned at university and provided valuable practical insights.

### 4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities

The program provided numerous opportunities to strengthen both technical and soft skills. I gained hands-on experience with AWS services, attended seminars and workshops, conducted self-study through official documentation, collaborated with teammates, and received constructive feedback from my mentor throughout the project. These experiences significantly improved my problem-solving abilities and independent learning skills.

### 5. Culture and Teamwork

The program fostered a positive and collaborative culture. Team members were approachable, supportive, and always willing to share their knowledge. Although everyone worked professionally, the environment remained friendly and comfortable, allowing interns to confidently ask questions and contribute to discussions.

### 6. Internship Experience

Overall, I am very satisfied with the internship experience. In addition to working on a real-world project, I had the opportunity to participate in seminars, workshops, and technical sharing sessions that broadened my understanding of Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence.

# Additional Questions

### What did you enjoy the most during your internship?

The highlight of my internship was the outstanding support from my mentor. Whenever I faced challenges, my mentor responded quickly and provided detailed guidance, allowing me to solve problems independently while learning from the process. I also enjoyed learning and working with AWS services, which introduced me to the fascinating world of Cloud Computing and modern cloud technologies.

### What do you think could be improved for future interns?

Overall, I am very satisfied with the program. As a suggestion, I believe it would be beneficial to include more learning sessions focused on **AI Security** and secure AI application development, as these topics are becoming increasingly important in today's technology landscape.

### Would you recommend this program to your friends? Why?

Yes. I would definitely recommend the First Cloud AI Journey program because it provides valuable opportunities to understand cloud infrastructure, AWS services, and real-world software development practices. It is an excellent program for students who are interested in Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and modern technologies.

# Suggestions and Future Expectations

### Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?

I would like to see more technical workshops and seminars focusing on **AI Security**, **Cloud Security**, and real-world cybersecurity scenarios on AWS. These topics would help interns gain a broader understanding of secure cloud application development.

### Would you like to continue participating in this program in the future?

Yes. If given the opportunity, I would be delighted to continue participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program. I hope to further enhance my technical expertise, contribute to more real-world projects, and continue developing my skills in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence.

### Additional Comments

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to all the mentors and the FCAJ team for their continuous support throughout the internship. Their guidance, encouragement, and willingness to share practical knowledge have greatly contributed to both my technical growth and professional development. I hope the program continues to grow and provides even more opportunities for future students to gain valuable industry experience with AWS technologies.
