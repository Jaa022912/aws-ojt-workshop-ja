# Event Report: Cloud Architect Competition

## Event Objective

The **Cloud Architect Competition** was an academic event organized for AWS interns to provide an engaging environment for reviewing and testing knowledge of **Cloud Computing**, **AWS Services**, and **Cloud Architecture** through a team-based competition.

The objectives of the event were:

- Reinforce knowledge of AWS and Cloud Architecture.
- Improve teamwork and collaboration skills.
- Enhance critical thinking and problem-solving abilities under time constraints.
- Encourage networking and knowledge sharing among internship teams.

## Competition Format

The competition followed a **head-to-head elimination format**, where two teams competed against each other in each match.

Each team consisted of **3 to 5 members** and answered a set of **10 questions** with increasing levels of difficulty.

Key rules of the competition included:

- Matches were conducted in a knockout format.
- Each question set contained 10 questions.
- If both teams finished with the same score, an **11th tie-breaker question** was used to determine the winner.
- The team that answered the tie-breaker correctly and the fastest advanced to the next round.

To make the competition more strategic, each team could use two special abilities during the game.

### ⭐ Hope Star

- Correct answer: Earn **double points**.
- Incorrect answer: Lose **double points**.

### 🛡️ Minimum Risk

- Incorrect answer: No points deducted.
- Correct answer: Receive only **50%** of the question's points.

Choosing the right moment to activate these abilities required careful discussion and teamwork.

## Competition Highlights

Throughout the event, the questions covered a wide range of AWS and Cloud Computing topics, including:

- AWS Core Services
- Cloud Architecture
- Networking
- Security
- Databases
- Storage
- Compute Services
- AWS Best Practices
- AWS Well-Architected Framework

Each team was given a limited amount of time to discuss and submit a final answer.

Besides technical knowledge, the competition emphasized teamwork, communication, and quick decision-making under pressure.

## What I Learned

Participating in this event helped me gain valuable technical knowledge as well as practical soft skills.

### Technical Knowledge

- Reviewed many AWS services that I had previously learned.
- Improved my understanding of applying cloud concepts to real-world scenarios.
- Strengthened my knowledge of AWS architecture design principles.

### Soft Skills

- Worked effectively as part of a team under time pressure.
- Improved communication and collaboration when discussing technical questions.
- Learned to make decisions based on the team's confidence level.
- Better understood how to assign tasks according to each member's strengths.

### Strategic Thinking

One of the most interesting aspects of the competition was deciding when to use the **Hope Star** and **Minimum Risk** abilities.

Our team needed to carefully consider:

- When it was worth taking risks to earn double points.
- When it was safer to avoid losing points.

This made the competition much more than a knowledge test, as it also evaluated teamwork and strategic decision-making.

## My Experience at the Event

The Cloud Architect Competition created an exciting and energetic atmosphere.

Every team prepared seriously and actively discussed each question before submitting their answers. The later questions became increasingly challenging, requiring both technical knowledge and effective teamwork.

Besides reviewing AWS concepts, I had the opportunity to observe how other teams analyzed problems, developed strategies, and decided when to use their special abilities. These experiences were valuable and helped broaden my perspective on collaborative problem-solving.

The event also allowed me to interact with many interns from different teams, exchange knowledge, and build new connections within the AWS internship community.
